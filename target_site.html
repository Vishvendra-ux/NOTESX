About GATE CSE Subject-wise Practice

Subject-wise practice combines every previous year question from a single GATE CSE subject — such as Algorithms or Operating Systems — into one continuous quiz. This is useful once you’ve already built familiarity with individual chapters and want to test recall and speed across the full subject, closer to how it appears in the actual GATE exam.

Every question here comes with a full worked solution, so subject-wise practice doubles as revision — you’re not just testing yourself, you’re relearning the method wherever you get stuck. For the full GATE CSE exam pattern, marking scheme, and subject-wise weightage, see the GATE CSE hub page.

Built and maintained by a team of GATE rankers, on a mission to make quality GATE preparation free and accessible for every aspirant. Maintained for GATE 2027 aspirants.

What is subject-wise practice on PracticePaper.in?

Subject-wise practice combines every previous year question from one full GATE CSE subject, such as Algorithms, into a single continuous quiz — useful for broader revision once individual chapters feel solid.

Should I use subject-wise or topic-wise practice first?

Start with topic-wise practice to build chapter-level fundamentals, then move to subject-wise practice to test recall and speed across the entire subject together.

How many subject-wise PYQs are available for GATE CSE?

GATE CSE subject-wise practice covers every core subject plus General Aptitude, alongside a curated Mock Test series and two ISRO-only electives — Software Engineering and Web Technology.

How many years of GATE CSE previous year questions should I practice?

Practicing at least the last 15–25 years of GATE CSE PYQs is recommended to understand recurring patterns and frequently tested topics across subjects.

Is PracticePaper.in updated for GATE 2027?

Yes, all GATE CSE subject-wise questions and solutions on this page are maintained for GATE 2027 aspirants and reflect the current exam pattern.

Do subject-wise quizzes include worked solutions?

Yes, every question in a GATE CSE subject-wise quiz comes with a full worked solution, so you can review the method behind each answer, not just whether you got it right.

Can I retake a subject-wise quiz to track my improvement?

Yes, you can retake any GATE CSE subject-wise quiz as many times as you like to track your improvement over time.
